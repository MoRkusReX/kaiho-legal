Privacy Policy

Effective September 24, 2026

Your photos stay on your device. Kaiho Photo does not upload your photos for editing, and it does not include advertising, analytics, or automatic crash-reporting services.

Who we are

This policy applies to the Kaiho Photo Android application (package com.kaihophoto.app), provided by Kaiho Photo. Questions or privacy requests can be sent to KaihoPhoto@outlook.com.

Information the app accesses

  • Photos and image files: Kaiho accesses only the images you select or share with the app, or images available through photo-library permission when you grant it. This access is used to preview, edit, export, and share your work.
  • Image metadata: Selected files may contain EXIF information such as camera and lens details, date, and location. Kaiho can display this information and, when “Keep EXIF metadata” is enabled, copy it into an exported image.
  • App settings and creations: Editing preferences, calibration settings, looks, recipes, signatures, purchase entitlement, and session information may be stored locally so features work as expected.
  • Optional feedback: If you choose Send feedback, your email app receives the message you write. When you choose to include diagnostics, the attachment can contain the app version, device model, Android version, GPU and memory information, selected file names and sizes, editing settings, processing timings, and error details. It does not contain your photos. You can turn off the attachment before continuing.

How information is used

The information above is used only to provide photo editing and export features, remember your choices, verify Pro access, diagnose issues you voluntarily report, and respond to your messages.

On-device processing

Photo editing, subject detection, masks, and AI denoising run locally on your device. Kaiho does not send your photos to the developer or to an AI server for processing.

Sharing and third-party services

Kaiho does not sell personal information. Information leaves the app only when you initiate an action or when needed for a service you request:

  • Google Play: Google Play processes purchases and provides Kaiho with purchase and entitlement status. Kaiho does not receive your full payment-card details. Google handles this information under its own privacy policy.
  • Android sharing: When you export, share a photo or recipe, or send diagnostics, the destination app you select receives that content and handles it under its own terms.
  • Email feedback: Feedback is sent through your chosen email provider to Kaiho Photo. The email address, message, and any diagnostic attachment you send are received by us.

Storage, retention, and deletion

Project data, settings, working files, and temporary files are stored on your device. Temporary data may be cleared by Kaiho or Android. You can delete app data through Android settings or remove it by uninstalling the app. Exported photos remain wherever you saved them until you delete them.

Feedback emails and attachments are retained only as long as reasonably necessary to respond, investigate the reported issue, and maintain related records. You may request deletion by emailing us from the same address used for the message. Purchase records retained by Google are controlled by Google and applicable legal requirements.

Security

Kaiho limits access to selected images and stores private app data within Android’s app storage. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, but we use reasonable safeguards appropriate to the information handled.

Children’s privacy

Kaiho is a general-audience photo editor and is not directed specifically to children. We do not knowingly collect children’s personal information through the app.

Your choices

You control which photos you open, whether EXIF metadata is retained, what you share, whether diagnostics are attached to feedback, and whether you send an email. Android settings let you review or revoke photo permissions.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when Kaiho’s features or legal obligations change. The effective date at the top will be revised when an update is published.

Contact

For support, privacy questions, access requests, or deletion requests, email KaihoPhoto@outlook.com.
