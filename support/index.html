Support

Help with Kaiho Photo for Android

Need help? Email us and include a short description of what happened, the file format you were editing, and your device model.

Contact

Email KaihoPhoto@outlook.com. Support is provided in English.

Optional diagnostics

Kaiho can prepare a diagnostic report when you choose to send feedback. Review the attachment before sending it. Diagnostic reports do not include your photos, and attaching one is optional.

Purchases

Purchases and subscriptions are processed by Google Play. Use Kaiho’s purchase screen to restore or verify access. Billing cancellations and payment-method changes are managed through Google Play.

Before contacting support

  • Confirm that Kaiho and Android are up to date.
  • Keep the original photo until the issue is resolved.
  • For purchase issues, connect to the internet and use Restore purchases / Verify now.
